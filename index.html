Channel 01 · Performance Ads
01

Performance Ads

One budget book for the whole portfolio, with spend set by property type instead of spread evenly.

  • Flats run lean. ₹15,000 a month each, every listing tuned to its own cost per lead between ₹115 and ₹185.
  • The villa carries more. ₹40,000 a month at a ₹350 target, for fewer, higher-value enquiries.
  • The plot sits between. ₹35,000 a month at ₹80 per lead for land buyers.
  • Overlap is planned. Listings in the same neighbourhood share audience rules so they never bid against each other.

Channel 04 · Retargeting & WhatsApp
04

Retargeting & WhatsApp

Most buyers don’t enquire on the first visit. Everyone who watched a reel, opened a form or visited the site is followed up, on Instagram and on WhatsApp, until they book a visit or opt out.

  1. Min 0Enquiry landsThe lead goes to the CRM and gets an instant WhatsApp reply with the brochure.
  2. Day 1Reel retargetPeople who watched 50% of a walkthrough see the floor-plan carousel.
  3. Day 3Site-visit slotsA WhatsApp message offers three weekend slots to pick from.
  4. Day 7Proof and urgencyA resident testimonial and the units left in their budget.
  5. Day 14Re-engageQuiet leads get a new project that matches their budget.
